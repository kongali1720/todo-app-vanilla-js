# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy toward other contributors.
- Helping newcomers learn and grow.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or insulting comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Spam, trolling, or intentionally disruptive behavior.
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional environment.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

Examples include:

- GitHub repositories
- Discussions
- Issue tracker
- Pull Requests
- Social media
- Community events

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by opening a private security report or contacting the project maintainer.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to take any action they deem appropriate, including:

- Warning
- Temporary suspension
- Permanent ban
- Removal of content
- Closing issues or pull requests
